Support

Status of the app

4D Moment is at the test stage: the planned first test version (a title screen only) is specified but not yet built, and no version has been released. There is no download yet.

How to reach us

Questions, problems or feedback: write to support@4d-moment.ch.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I download 4D Moment?

Nowhere yet. No version has been built or released; this site will link the app once a first test version exists.

What will the first test version do?

It is specified to show a title screen only: no camera access, no data collection, no network connections, no account.

Does 4D Moment send my photos or videos anywhere?

The first test version captures nothing at all. Later capture versions are designed to compute everything on the phone and to keep camera images and results on the phone unless you share them yourself; see the privacy policy.

Which phones will be supported?

iPhone and Android are planned to be supported, in the same version.
